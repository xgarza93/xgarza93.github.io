Privacy policy

heb-inventory is a personal script used only by its owner, Xavier Garza.

It reads H-E-B receipt emails in the owner's own Gmail account (read-only) and updates one document in the owner's own Google Drive. Product names from receipts may be sent to the Anthropic API to label new grocery items.

No data is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
